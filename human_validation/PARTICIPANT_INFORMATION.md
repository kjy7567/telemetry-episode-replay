# Participant Information and Consent

## Purpose

This study examines whether controlled building-telemetry benchmark interactions resemble requests and follow-ups used in building operations. It does not evaluate the participant's job performance.

## What participation involves

You will receive nine structured telemetry intents. For each intent, you will first write the request and follow-ups you would realistically use without seeing benchmark wording. You will then review the corresponding benchmark interaction and provide categorical ratings and comments. Expected completion time should be communicated by the study coordinator before participation.

## Data collected

The study records a pseudonymous participant ID, broad role category, years of relevant experience, authored text, ratings, issue codes, and optional comments. Do not enter your name, employer, email address, building name, customer name, or any operationally sensitive information.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop before submitting your responses or ask the study coordinator to remove your responses before aggregate analysis. Declining has no penalty. Responses are analyzed only after affirmative consent to participate.

## Publication and quotation

Aggregate results may be reported in a paper, author response, appendix, or released research artifact. Verbatim authored text is included only when `consent_to_quote` is `yes`; otherwise it contributes only to aggregate statistics. The study coordinator must remove accidental identifying details before analysis or release.

## Consent fields

Set `consent_to_participate` to `yes` only after reading this information and agreeing to participate. Set `consent_to_quote` independently to `yes` or `no`.

The study coordinator is responsible for completing any ethics review, exemption determination, or other institutional requirement that applies before collecting responses.
